# Idea

## Domain
A photo-sharing site: people make an account, create posts with one or more photos, and comment on (and like) any post.

## User story
**As a** hobby photographer, **I need to** share sets of photos and see what other people think of them,
**so that** I can get feedback, improve, and find photographers whose work I like.

## Questions the app answers
- What are the newest posts, and which match what I'm searching for (caption text or username)?
- What has a particular photographer posted?
- What are people saying about a post (threaded comments), and how many people liked it?
